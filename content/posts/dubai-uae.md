  Dubai's heat is real, but its response to that heat — enormous indoor malls, theme parks, and aquariums — happens to solve exactly the kind of midday meltdown problem parents dread on a beach trip elsewhere.

  ## Structuring a Day Around Heat

  Mornings are for outdoor time: beaches, parks, or a desert safari departing early. Midday shifts indoors to a mall, aquarium, or indoor theme park, and evenings reopen outdoor options like the Dubai Fountain show or a walk through Al Seef.

  ## Getting Around

  The Dubai Metro is modern, air-conditioned, and stroller-accessible, but taxis are inexpensive enough that most families use a mix of both depending on distance and nap timing.

  ## A Note on Logistics

  English is widely spoken, signage is bilingual, and hotel staff are accustomed to family requests — cribs, bottle warming, and connecting rooms are usually simple to arrange with advance notice.
