  Crete offers the classic Greek island experience — whitewashed villages, turquoise water, unhurried tavernas — at a fraction of the cost of the more famous Cyclades islands like Santorini, while being noticeably easier to get around with kids thanks to its size and direct flight connections.

  ## Choosing a Base

  Chania and Rethymno both have walkable old towns with harbor-front dining and are close to several excellent family beaches. Renting a car opens up quieter coves further from the main tourist strip.

  ## A Typical Family Day

  Mornings at the beach, a long lakeside or harbor-front lunch during the heat of the day (and a nap back at the room or on a shaded lounger), then an early evening walk through town before a relaxed dinner — Cretan restaurants rarely rush families out.

  ## Value for Money

  Compared to Italy, France, or Spain in peak season, Crete's accommodation, car rentals, and dining costs are meaningfully lower, making it a strong choice for longer stays or larger families.
