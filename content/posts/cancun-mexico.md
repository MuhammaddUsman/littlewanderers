Cancun is the default answer for a reason: the all-inclusive model removes the two biggest stressors of family travel — deciding where to eat and staying within budget once you've arrived, since meals, drinks, and often activities are already paid for.

## Picking the Right Resort

Family-specific resorts (as opposed to adults-only or party-focused ones) typically advertise kids' clubs, splash pads, and connecting or multi-room suites. Read reviews specifically from other parents — noise levels and pool depth vary enormously between properties that look similar in photos.

## Hotel Zone vs. Downtown

The Hotel Zone (Zona Hotelera) has the big all-inclusive resorts and calmer lagoon-side water; downtown Cancun is cheaper and more local but requires more independent planning for meals and activities.

## Day Trips Worth the Effort

If your kids handle a travel day well, Xcaret or Xel-Há are worth leaving the resort for — both are designed as full-day, food-included parks so you don't lose the all-inclusive convenience entirely.
