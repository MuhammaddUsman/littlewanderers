content: |
  Chiang Mai moves at a gentler pace than Bangkok, with a walkable Old City, a strong expat and family-travel community, and prices low enough that a week here often costs less than a long weekend in a major Western city.

  ## Where to Stay

  The Old City (inside the old moat walls) puts you within walking distance of temples, markets, and cafes. Families wanting a pool often choose a guesthouse just outside the walls, still a short tuk-tuk ride from everything.

  ## Booking Elephant Experiences

  Not all "sanctuaries" are equal — look for ones that explicitly prohibit riding and focus on feeding, walking alongside, and bathing elephants. These tend to book up, especially in high season, so reserve a few weeks ahead.

  ## Getting Around

  Songthaews (shared red trucks) are the cheap, everyday way to get around and easily fit a family with luggage. For day trips outside the city, hiring a private driver for the day is affordable and removes the stress of navigating unfamiliar roads.
