London's reputation as an expensive, adult-oriented city undersells how much of it is genuinely built for families — and a surprising amount of the best of it, the major museums specifically, is completely free.

## The Free Museum Advantage

The Natural History Museum, British Museum, Science Museum, and V&A are all free to enter, each with dedicated family trails, activity sheets, and interactive galleries aimed squarely at kids. This alone can anchor several days of a trip without ticket costs piling up.

## Where to Stay

South Kensington puts you within walking distance of three major museums and Hyde Park, while areas near King's Cross offer easy rail connections if a Harry Potter Studio Tour day trip (in nearby Watford) is on the list.

## Managing a City Break

London rewards a slower pace — pick one museum or park per day rather than stacking sights, since distances between areas are larger than they look on a tourist map, and London's famous unpredictable weather can turn an ambitious outdoor day into a scramble for cover.
