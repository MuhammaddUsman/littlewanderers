kidFriendlyAttractions:
- Petra — walking or riding a horse cart through the Siq to the Treasury is a genuine wow moment for kids
- Floating in the Dead Sea — a novelty that delights almost every age group
- Wadi Rum desert camps — jeep tours and a night in a Bedouin-style tent under clear skies
- Amman Citadel and the Roman Theater — compact ruins that are easy to explore in an hour
- Ma'in Hot Springs — waterfalls of warm mineral water, fun for older kids to swim near
recommendedAges: Best for ages 6 and up who can manage walking on uneven ancient terrain; younger kids do fine with a carrier
safetyTips:
- Petra involves significant walking on uneven stone — bring sturdy shoes and pace the visit across a full day
- Dead Sea water is extremely salty and stings eyes badly — keep young kids' faces out of the water and rinse promptly
- Desert temperatures swing widely between day and night — pack layers even in warmer months
- Jordan is politically stable and considered safe for tourism, but check current government travel advisories before booking
strollerFriendly: Challenging
napTimeTips: Petra and Wadi Rum are not stroller terrain — a hiking carrier is essential for kids under about 4. Build in a rest day at a Dead Sea resort between adventure days; these hotels have pools, air conditioning, and calm settings well suited to naps.
pickyEaterFood:
- Hummus and pita bread — mild, familiar, and available at every meal
